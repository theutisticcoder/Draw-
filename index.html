Spectral Mixing & Pressure Dynamics

Real oil paint is a physical medium governed by fluid dynamics and pigment interaction. DrawPaw's spectral color engine uses subtractive RYB/spectral transitions so Yellow + Blue produces rich, radiant greens, Red + Yellow forms luminous cadmium oranges, and heavy brush pressure displaces paint volume dynamically.
