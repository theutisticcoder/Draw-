Subtractive RYB Color & Pressure Mechanics

Unlike standard digital paint software that calculates additive RGB light (producing murky gray or purple when mixing yellow and blue), DrawPaw utilizes a true subtractive Red-Yellow-Blue (RYB) pigment interpolation model combined with real-time pressure dynamics.

Subtractive RYB Synthesis

Trilinear interpolation through artist pigment space. Yellow and Blue produce emerald greens; Red and Yellow produce vibrant oranges.

Hardware & Velocity Pressure
