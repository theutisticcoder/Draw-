Studio Control Matrix

Pressure-Sensitive Blending & Gestures

  • Stylus Force: Modulates stroke width, impasto height, and wet color pickup/blending depth.
  • Two-Finger Pinch & Drag: Smoothly pinch to zoom in/out and drag with two fingers to pan across the canvas.
  • Blend Smudge Mode (B): Uses a dry/clean brush to smear and blend existing canvas paints without adding new pigment.
  • Stationary Hold (0.75s): Hold still without moving to sample a color with the Eyedropper.
